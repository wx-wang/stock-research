# Data Requirements

## Mandatory Upstream Data

- current Industry Thesis and Profit Pool conclusions;
- Company Alpha, growth drivers, competitive risks, and falsifiers;
- upstream Industry Valuation Adapter, market narrative, narrative-to-numbers assumptions, preliminary priced-in industry assumptions, and relevant Q6 Unknowns;
- market state and liquidity context;
- as-of date.

If the causal path from industry to company earnings is absent, return Recheck.

The upstream Q6 record is contextual. Company valuation still requires company-specific business mix, earnings, cash flow, capital structure, price, consensus, and implied-expectation work.

## Financial and Operating Data

- a dated `Financial Statement Evidence Packet` when a current filing is material, including period/definition normalization, source coverage, earnings-quality reconciliation, accounting caveats, and Unknowns; or equivalent raw inputs sufficient to create it first;
- historical revenue by material segment;
- margins, profit, and cash flow;
- volume, price, market share, product mix, cost, and expense drivers;
- company guidance and its publication date;
- relevant balance-sheet or capital-structure items needed for valuation;
- operating data needed to verify the growth bridge.

## Market Expectation Data

- analyst consensus with observation date and comparable accounting period;
- material range or dispersion when available;
- market narrative with identifiable evidence;
- current price-implied expectation if it can be responsibly inferred;
- original expectation baseline for update tasks.
- dated pre-event expectations for material results, product launches, orders, guidance, or catalysts;
- post-event consensus/narrative revisions and their direction, magnitude, and observation date when available;
- the next decision-relevant metric, expected range or direction, and verification window.

If consensus cannot be identified, do not Pass an earnings-variant conclusion.

If the thesis depends on temporarily depressed earnings, also require evidence for business quality and Company Alpha, the temporary-versus-structural diagnosis, balance-sheet and cash-flow runway, leading operating indicators, the old earnings path currently implied by price, the verification date, and a falsifier.

## Valuation and Liquidity Data

- current price and date;
- dynamic PE on a stated earnings basis (next-year or current-year, explicitly labeled) for terminal-ceiling reverse calculation;
- share-count change history when dilution or buybacks are material, for per-share basis checks;
- share count, market capitalization, and material capital structure;
- current valuation measures relevant to the company;
- historical valuation on a comparable basis;
- relevant peer or industry comparison with definition consistency;
- trading volume and liquidity needed to assess entry and exit;
- market regime and risk-appetite context.

Without a current price and consistent valuation inputs, do not Pass valuation.

## Scenario Requirements

For Bull, Base, and Bear cases state:

- demand or volume;
- price;
- revenue;
- margin;
- profit;
- cash-flow direction;
- duration;
- terminal-profit multiple (m: terminal profit versus current profit) implied by each scenario, for comparison with the price-implied ceiling;
- value-range implication;
- validation event and time.

Avoid unsupported precision. When only directional data exist, use ranges or qualitative conclusions and lower confidence.
