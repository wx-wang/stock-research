# Data Requirements

## Mandatory Baseline

Require an original or latest Investment Memo, or an equivalent record containing:

- research subject, market, and original as-of date;
- one-sentence thesis and value-creation chain;
- stage conclusions and confidence;
- supporting facts;
- key assumptions;
- falsification conditions;
- verification metrics and expected direction;
- catalysts and expected timing;
- earnings view and validation timeline;
- valuation, risk reward, and liquidity conclusion;
- trend and action conditions;
- recommended state and upgrade/downgrade/exit conditions.
- dated Expectation Position Record by horizon, including the preserved pre-event baseline, descriptive phase, revision path, next surprise test, and falsifier;
- when an industry thesis is material: Industry Scope Card, Six-Question Industry Card, Cross-Question Constraint Map, and Six-Question Monitoring Baseline.

Without this baseline, return Recheck.

## New Information

Use only facts available by the update as-of date. Relevant categories include:

- for a new quarterly, interim, or annual filing: a dated `Financial Statement Evidence Packet` or equivalent normalized delta with source, period, units, restatement and consolidation-scope definitions;
- industry demand, supply, price, inventory, capacity, policy, or technology;
- six-question deltas: Q1 size/growth/cycle, Q2 map/profit distribution, Q3 segment economics, Q4 technology, Q5 regulation/geopolitics/localization, or Q6 industry valuation/narrative;
- value-chain margins, market share, entrants, or exits;
- company product, customer, order, share, cost, price, or execution;
- financial results, guidance, earnings expectations, and cash flow;
- actual result versus the preserved pre-event expectation, post-event forecast/narrative revisions, and their observation dates;
- current price, valuation, liquidity, and trend;
- catalyst realization, delay, or failure;
- investor-provided field observations or concerns, labeled by evidence type.

## Comparison Record

For each material item capture:

- original expectation;
- expected observation date;
- actual result;
- variance direction and magnitude when available;
- expectation revision direction and whether it is accelerating, stable, peaking, or reversing;
- affected assumption, falsifier, or metric;
- earliest affected skill;
- affected six-question record and material cross-question propagation;
- downstream implications.

## Minimum Decision Data

- Strengthened requires new evidence tied to a named original assumption.
- Unchanged requires actual evidence consistent with the expected path, not merely no news.
- Weakened requires an identified negative variance that has not yet falsified the thesis.
- Invalidated requires a triggered falsification condition or a broken core causal link.

If the current state cannot be updated until another skill reruns, label the state implication provisional and return Recheck.
