# Data Requirements

## Mandatory Upstream Data

- validated Industry Thesis or an equivalent current conclusion;
- validated Profit Pool location;
- beneficiary-segment economics and winner characteristics;
- exact Industry Map node and profit-pool mechanism;
- segment-level Q3 advantage hypothesis, expected competitive response, and relevant six-question Unknowns;
- industry catalysts, risks, falsification conditions, and verification metrics;
- as-of date.

Without a profit-pool position, return Recheck.

## Company Business Data

- a dated `Financial Statement Evidence Packet` when available for segment economics, operating-driver changes, cash conversion, and peer-comparable definitions;
- products and services;
- customers and applications;
- segment revenue and profit sources;
- company value-chain position;
- revenue model and material business-model changes;
- volume, price, product mix, costs, margins, and cash flow;
- capacity or operational constraints when material.

## Competitive Evidence

- market share and change over time;
- major competitors and comparable operating results;
- technology evidence and commercialization results;
- cost evidence rather than cost claims;
- customer retention, concentration, qualification, or switching evidence;
- scale evidence and its economic effect;
- brand evidence and pricing effect;
- evidence that a claimed advantage persists through competition.

## Growth and Validation Data

- market growth versus company share growth;
- volume and price changes;
- product-mix changes;
- margin and cash-flow changes;
- orders, customers, products, or operational catalysts;
- expected verification period.

When the thesis depends on currently weak earnings, also require the cause and duration of the weakness, evidence that the Alpha mechanism remains intact, leading operating indicators that should improve first, and the balance-sheet/cash-flow items needed downstream to test survival through the verification window. If the weakness may be structural and the distinction is unresolved, return Recheck.

## Minimum Pass Data

A Pass requires evidence for:

1. beneficiary value-chain position;
2. at least one relative competitive advantage;
3. a revenue and profit transmission mechanism;
4. cash-flow direction;
5. durability and a falsification condition.

If the company benefits but relative advantage is unknown, return Recheck rather than Company Alpha Pass.
