# Data Requirements

## Research Scope

Required:

- A-share, Hong Kong, or specified subset;
- industry universe, company universe, or named subject;
- as-of date;
- investor question and decision context;
- comparison period or expectation baseline.

For an industry candidate, also record the preliminary `Industry Scope Card`:

- final product/service and material substitute boundary;
- technology path;
- geographic and market boundary;
- value-chain position;
- lifecycle and competition-structure context;
- units, inclusion/exclusion rules, and definitions.

For opportunity-discovery requests also record:

- scan mode: Snapshot / Rolling Window / Theme Deep Scan;
- time window and page count;
- Knowledge Star keyword set and de-duplicated topic count;
- number of topic details, IMA items, reports and primary-source pages read;
- candidate granularity and whether subsegment decomposition is required;
- sources not covered and their effect on confidence.

## Market Regime Data

Required categories:

- interest-rate direction;
- U.S. dollar and relevant currency environment;
- credit conditions;
- market fund-flow direction;
- index price state;
- total turnover and its change;
- market breadth;
- style and risk-appetite change.

Each item should identify observation period, publication or availability time, comparison basis, source identity, and any definition caveat.

## Opportunity Signal Data

### Industry

- technology or product change;
- cost change;
- policy change;
- demand or consumption change;
- supply, capacity, inventory, or price change;
- penetration or application change;
- industry earnings-expectation change when available;
- sector valuation, attention, and trend as preliminary pricing signals.

### Company

- revenue, profit, margin, or cash-flow change;
- orders, volume, price, product mix, or market-share change;
- company announcement or operational catalyst;
- valuation, trading attention, and trend as preliminary signals.

## Opportunity Hypothesis Minimum

Before handing a candidate to Industry Value Chain Analyzer, record:

- a dated trigger fact;
- the change versus a named baseline;
- a preliminary value-migration and profit-pool hypothesis;
- a possible business and earnings/FCF transmission;
- Market View versus Agent View;
- one verification metric or event and date;
- an explicit evidence level: Radar Only, Narrative Extracted, or Cross-Validated.

For an industry candidate also record a `Six-Question Rough Cut`:

- Q1: available size/growth/cycle signal and definition caveat;
- Q2: preliminary nodes and profit-pool hypothesis;
- Q3: preliminary segment-economics or profit-retention mechanism;
- Q4: current or next technology route that could change the thesis;
- Q5: material regulation, geopolitics, or localization issue;
- Q6: preliminary valuation context and identifiable market narrative;
- evidence level and critical Unknown for each material question;
- Dominant Driver and Recommended Deep-Dive Order.

This rough cut is a routing record. It does not need the depth required for an Industry Thesis or Profit Pool Pass.

## Minimum Pass Data

A candidate cannot Pass without:

1. one dated, verifiable new fact;
2. a comparison showing what changed;
3. a plausible business and earnings transmission hypothesis;
4. a reason the change may be under-recognized;
5. a verification metric or event.

For an industry candidate, the scope must also be coherent enough to hand off, and the six-question rough cut must expose any unresolved issue that could reverse the preliminary value-migration hypothesis.

If any item is missing, return Recheck and name the missing item. If the object is a broad heterogeneous industry, return `Recheck—Subsegment` before a sector-wide Reject. Do not fill gaps with unsupported estimates.
